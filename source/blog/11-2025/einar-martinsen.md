![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/11/image-6.png)[Einar Martinsen](https://www.einarmartinsen.com) is a VFX Art Director with a distinct and unmistakable style to his art - whether digital shots or plein air oil paintings.

He has worked on many major motion pictures and TV shows, with credits to his name such as Avengers: End Game, Lord of the Rings: Rings of Power, among others. 

The Foundry invited Einar to create the splash screens for their new Nuke family of products. As a nod to Nuke itself, he chose to embody the spirit of this software in this magnificent "Node Tree".

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/11/image-4.png)As this epic vista deals with expansive environments, he chose Gaea to create the terrains for this world.

> *"Using Gaea for this project was great as it served as a really strong foundation to build out the fantasy world. The erosion and flexibility Gaea offers is both fun and yield amazing results!   

>   

> Gaea is intuitive and I only scratched the surface of it so far! I'm looking forward to dive deeper with it and use it for VFX and concept art alike!"   

>   

> – Einar Martinsen*

He has kindly shared several of these images along with a behind-the-scenes story on how he created Nuke 16's splash screens. Watch the video below.
