# Community Videos

This section collects videos created by the broader Gaea community. These videos are shared as learning resources and points of reference, but they are **not** official documentation and may reflect personal workflows, opinions, older UI, or third-party tools.


## Authorized Training Partners

Some videos are produced by **Authorized Training Partners**. These entries are marked with a seal icon: <i class="fa-duotone fa-solid fa-certificate toc-icon"></i>

* Seal indicates the creator is an approved training partner, not that every tip is official product policy.
* Content may still differ by Gaea version; check the video’s publish date and the Gaea version shown.

## What you’ll find here

* Beginner walkthroughs and fundamentals
* Node techniques and graph breakdowns
* Production workflows and case studies
* Optimization, build strategies, and troubleshooting
* Integration with other tools (DCCs, engines, renderers)

## Want to submit a video?

If you have a video you’d like listed here:

* Include the **video link**, **creator name**, and a **one-line summary**.
* Mention the **Gaea version** used (or the release timeframe).
* Add tags (e.g., *Erosion, Texturing, Export, UE, Houdini, Automation*).
* If you are an Authorized Training Partner, include your partner name so the seal can be applied.
* Contact [https://quadspinner.com/support](Support)


## Disclaimer

* Community videos are **not** endorsements by QuadSpinner.
* Information may be **incomplete, outdated, or incorrect**.
* Always verify critical steps against the official manual, release notes, and in-app tooltips.
* Some videos may include third-party assets, plugins, or pipelines not supported by QuadSpinner.
* If a video suggests license, security, or installation workarounds, treat it as informational only and validate with official guidance.
