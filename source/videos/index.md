# Video Learning Material

This section is the video library for learning Gaea through visual walkthroughs. It includes both **official QuadSpinner videos** and **community-created videos**, organized so you can quickly find either a focused technique or a complete start-to-finish workflow.

## Official videos

* **Official overviews**: feature introductions, UI tours, and release-oriented explanations.
* **Deep dives**: longer, methodical breakdowns of major systems and workflows.
* **Detailed tutorials**: end-to-end projects with context, settings, and reasoning.
* **Brief tutorials & techniques**: short, practical tips you can apply immediately.
* **Node videos**: individual node explanations, common use-cases, and best practices.

:::warning

Go to the <i class="fa-brands fa-youtube"></i> [QuadSpinner YouTube Channel](https://youtube.com/quadspinner) for the latest videos, and subscribe so you never miss new training content.

The content on this learning page can sometimes be updated a bit late and may not have the latest videos.

:::

## Community videos

* Tutorials, workflows, experiments, and niche techniques created by the Gaea community.
* These can be extremely useful for real-world problem solving, but may reflect personal workflows or older versions.

## Notes 

### Versioning

Gaea evolves quickly. A video may show UI, nodes, or defaults that differ from your build. When following a tutorial, always check:

* the **publish date**
* the **Gaea version** shown in the top-right of the interface (when available)
* any **release notes** that affect the demonstrated feature

### Official vs community content

Official videos reflect QuadSpinner’s intended workflows and supported features. Community videos are provided for convenience and learning value, but are **not endorsements** and may include unsupported tools or assumptions. See the Community Videos page for details and disclaimers.
