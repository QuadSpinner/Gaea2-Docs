# Simulations

Gaea's wide range of simulations help you mimic nature through simulating real-world processes from a both scientific and artistic point-of-view.

![](/.data/covers/debris.webp)

The most important simulation is @erosion2 as well as the classic @erosion. These are augmented by @thermal2, @crumble, @sediments, and others.

![](/.data/covers/snow.webp)

@snow, @snowfield, and @dusting create snow simulations. While @icefloe can create floating chunks of ice.

@lake, @sea, and @rivers create various kinds of water simulations.

![](/.data/covers/veg.webp)

@trees and @shrubs powerful ways to create vegetation masks driven by real geological factors which can then be used later in DCC apps to create entire forests.

@debris creates thousands (even millions) of physics-enabled debris onto your terrain.
