# TextureBase

Creates a mask simulating natural material distribution onto which color from @satmap or @cluter or other color node is "flowed". Useful for driving texturing, shading, or erosion masks with a natural, layered look.

{% include "/.data/includes/color-lookup.md" %}

## When to Use It
Use TextureBase when you want a broad natural-distribution mask that gives SatMap, CLUT, or other color nodes a believable base to follow.
